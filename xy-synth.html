Help

Slide left and right for pitch, up and down for the filter. With Arp on, two or more notes run the arpeggio. With it off, they play as a chord.

Keep the same notes down for a couple of seconds and the arpeggio holds, so you can let go and change the sound. Tap Hold to stop it.

Each of the three synths has its own sound, scale and effects. Switch one to Steps to write a pattern that plays along with the beats.
