Website

The buttons at the top

First row: Live or Song, play and stop, the tempo (tap the number to change it), then the mixer, settings and help.

Second row: the keyboard and 1 2 3 are the synths; the drum and A B C are the drum machines. A dot shows which ones are playing, and flashes with each note. On a synth, the three buttons after the tabs pick Pad, Steps or Piano roll. The waves button at the end opens the effects.

Third row: the sound of the synth or drum machine on screen. Tap its name to change it and shape it. On a synth, the arpeggio and the scale (the steps button) follow. On the drums and on a synth in Steps or Piano roll, the bin clears the pattern on screen, and the next two buttons copy it and paste a copy over it (both ask first if steps would be lost). On the Sample kit it shows the loaded file, with Load and the loop's length next to it. On the drums, the patterns come next.

The pad

Slide left and right for pitch, up and down for the filter: brighter at the top of each keyboard, darker at the bottom.

Arpeggio and chords

With Arp on, two or more fingers run an arpeggio. With it off, they play a chord. The dots button next to it sets the arpeggio's speed, direction and how long to hold.

Keep the same notes down for a couple of seconds and Hold lights up: let go and the arpeggio keeps playing, so you can change the sound. Play a new chord to change it, or tap Hold to stop it. Change the scale while it's held and the notes move onto the new scale.

Three synths

1 2 3 are three synths, each with its own sound, scale and effects. Whatever one is playing carries on when you switch to another.

Steps

Switch a synth to Steps to write a pattern on a grid of its notes. Steps play along with the beats, so press play to hear them. Each synth has 32 patterns, 8 at a time: the down arrow shows 9 to 16 and so on, the up arrow goes back.

Piano roll

Piano roll shows the same patterns as Steps, but notes can be long. Press an empty spot to add a note, and drag right to make it longer. Drag the end of a note to stretch or shorten it, drag the rest of it to move it, and tap it to take it out. New notes are as long as the last one you drew.

Scroll the notes on the left to move up and down the keyboard. Press one to hear it; it plays for as long as you hold it. The roll covers four octaves from the lowest note; Steps shows the bottom two. In Steps, a long note lights its first step and shades the rest; tap any of them to take it out.

Drums

A B C are three drum machines. They all play together, each with its own kit, patterns and effects. Tap steps to add hits and switch between 32 patterns, 8 at a time: the down arrow shows 9 to 16 and so on, the up arrow goes back. An arrow is underlined when the pattern playing is that way. The name under the tabs shows the kit: 909, 808 or Sample; tap it to change. The Sample kit cuts a loop you load into 8 slices. It guesses whether the loop is 1, 2 or 4 bars long and fits it to the tempo; if it sounds too fast or slow, tap the bars button next to Load.

Sound and effects

Tap the sound's name to open its menu. A synth's has a list of sounds (bass, lead and pad), the envelope, the Filter and distortion. On a tablet held upright, the filter and distortion sit under the pad instead, next to the delay and reverb. The drums' has the kits, a Filter and distortion. Turn Cutoff down for a darker, duller sound, and Resonance up for a ringing edge. Knobs work by pressing one and sliding up or down. Effects (the waves button) has delay and reverb, for the synths and the drums. Tempo sets the speed of everything.

Mixer

The faders button opens the Mixer. A channel's name lights up while it plays and flashes with each note. Each has a level, M to mute, S to solo, how much reverb, delay and distortion each one gets, and its filter's cutoff. These are the same knobs as in the effects and sound menus.

Live and Song

In Live, play loops the patterns picked on each screen. In Song, it plays the song from the top, one row per bar, and goes round again at the end.

Each row has a slot for synths 1 2 3 and drums A B C. Tap a slot to pick a pattern, or Off. Tap a bar number to play from that bar: stopped, play starts there; playing, the song jumps there at the end of the bar. + Add a bar copies the last row; × removes one. In Song the synths play their patterns even in Pad mode, so you can still play the pad over the top.

While the song plays, 1 2 3 and A B C still open each synth and drum machine so you can change patterns. Tap Song to get back to the song.

Stop cuts all sound, echoes and reverb too, and stops any arpeggios.

Crackles?

Turn up Latency in Settings, the sliders button. The sound lags your fingers a little more but has more time to keep up.
