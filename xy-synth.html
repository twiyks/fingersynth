The buttons at the top

First row: Live or Song, play and stop, the tempo (tap the number to change it), then the mixer, settings and help.

Second row: the keyboard and 1 2 3 are the synths; the drum and A B C are the drum machines. A dot shows which ones are playing. On a synth, tap the keyboard again to pick its sound and shape it; on the drums, tap the drum again to pick the kit. The waves button at the end opens the effects.

Third row: on a synth, Pad or Steps, the arpeggio and the scale. On the drums, the patterns, Clear, and the kit.

The pad

Slide left and right for pitch, up and down for the filter: brighter at the top of each keyboard, darker at the bottom.

Arpeggio and chords

With Arp on, two or more fingers run an arpeggio. With it off, they play a chord. The dots button next to it sets the arpeggio's speed, direction and how long to hold.

Keep the same notes down for a couple of seconds and Hold lights up: let go and the arpeggio keeps playing, so you can change the sound. Play a new chord to change it, or tap Hold to stop it.

Three synths

1 2 3 are three synths, each with its own sound, scale and effects. Whatever one is playing carries on when you switch to another.

Steps

Switch a synth to Steps to write a pattern on a grid of its notes. Steps play along with the beats, so press play to hear them.

Drums

A B C are three drum machines. They all play together, each with its own kit, patterns and effects. Tap steps to add hits and switch between 8 patterns. The button after Clear shows the kit: 909, 808 or Sample; tap it to change. The Sample kit cuts a loop you load into 8 slices. It guesses whether the loop is 1, 2 or 4 bars long and fits it to the tempo; if it sounds too fast or slow, tap the bars button next to Load.

Sound and effects

A synth's menu (tap the keyboard while on a synth) has its sound, the envelope, filter and distortion. Effects has delay and reverb. Tempo sets the speed of everything.

Mixer

The faders button opens the Mixer: a level for each synth and drum machine, M to mute, S to solo, and how much reverb, delay and distortion each one gets. These are the same as the Amount sliders in the effects panels.

Live and Song

In Live, play loops the patterns picked on each screen. In Song, it plays the song from the top, one row per bar, and goes round again at the end.

Each row has a slot for synths 1 2 3 and drums A B C. Tap a slot to pick a pattern, or Off. + Add a bar copies the last row; × removes one. In Song the synths play their Steps patterns even in Pad mode, so you can still play the pad over the top.

While the song plays, 1 2 3 and A B C still open each synth and drum machine so you can change patterns. Tap Song to get back to the song.

Stop cuts all sound, echoes and reverb too, and stops any arpeggios.

Crackles?

Turn up Latency in Settings, the sliders button. The sound lags your fingers a little more but has more time to keep up.
